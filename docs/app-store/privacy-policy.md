# NOVEN Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-19

Noven is designed as a local-first habit and reflection app. This policy describes the first iOS build of Noven.

This same policy is available inside the app from the welcome screen and Settings.

## Data Stored On Your Device

Noven may store the following information locally on your device:

- Your chosen name
- Your MBTI type
- Your focus goal
- Habits you create
- Habit completion logs
- Reflections and notes you write
- Reminder preferences

This information is stored locally so the app can show your habits, reflections, and patterns.

## Data We Collect

This build of Noven does not collect your personal data on our servers.

The app does not include:

- Account sign-in
- Cloud sync
- Advertising SDKs
- Third-party tracking
- Server-side AI processing

Noven uses Adapty and Apple's purchase system for Premium subscriptions. This can process purchase status, product identifiers, and store-related subscription information. Habit, reflection, MBTI, focus, and reminder content is not sent to Adapty.

## Prompt Export

Noven Premium can generate a Markdown prompt from your local app data. This is only created when you choose to export it. Export uses the native iOS share sheet, and you decide where to send or save the text.

If you paste exported text into another app or service, that external app or service will handle the data under its own privacy policy.

## Notifications

Noven may ask for permission to send local habit reminders. Notifications are optional and can be disabled in the app settings or iOS Settings.

Noven does not send marketing notifications.

## Children

Noven is not intended to collect personal information from children. This build does not transmit user data to Noven servers.

## Changes

If future versions add accounts, sync, analytics, crash reporting, or server features beyond subscription purchase handling, this policy and the App Store privacy details must be updated before release.
